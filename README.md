## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Frontend Engineer specialized in React, Next.js and TypeScript**, focused on building SaaS products and complex business applications.

I currently work as the **primary frontend developer at Powip**, a production ERP SaaS platform used by approximately **800 users**. I work across the frontend architecture, feature development, UX/UI, performance, accessibility and API integration.

I've built and delivered complete product areas including:

- Operations management
- Sales workflows
- Automated onboarding and subscriptions
- Courier settlements
- Customer acquisition experiences

At **Devlusoft**, I built the frontend of a SaaS platform **from scratch**, covering inventory management, catalogs, ecommerce, orders, authentication, Mercado Libre integration, Excel imports, notifications and dropshipping workflows.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **engineering and product** — understanding the problem behind a feature, making pragmatic technical decisions and building interfaces that are scalable, accessible and easy to use.
